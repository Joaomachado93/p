    This page was made especially for Guloso Take Away by João Machado, a web developer here in Guimarães. It includes:

    • Fast mobile page with your name and identity
    • Menu and prices that are easy to update
    • Buttons to call, WhatsApp and get directions
    • Booking or order requests straight to WhatsApp
    • Your own domain (e.g. gulosotake.pt) linked to Google Maps
